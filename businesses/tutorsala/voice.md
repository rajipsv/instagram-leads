- **What I sell:** One platform for the business side of a tuition centre:
  - Education CRM: enquiries from website, WhatsApp and walk-ins, demos and

  - Parent communication: automated WhatsApp, email and SMS when attendance drops
    or test results are posted, plus a parent portal
  - Fees: see fees due and paid, monthly or per-class, without chasing anyone on
    the phone. Tuition is collected on the centre's own UPI or bank
  - Student success: homework, skills, interventions and progress, not just grades
  - Business intelligence: KPIs, trends, daily decisions
  - AI assistants for owners, tutors, parents and students: insights and message drafts
  - Setup done with you on a call: batches, teachers and fees added, students
    imported from Excel. Nothing to install
  - Marketplace listing so parents can find the centre and send an enquiry

## My positions

1. Teaching and running a tuition centre are two different jobs. Tutorsala does the
   second one, so you can do the first.

   The leaks are in enquiries, fees and parent updates.
3. A fee reminder should not need a phone call from you.
4. An "admissions open" post is only half the job. The enquiries it brings in need
   a place to go and a follow-up.
